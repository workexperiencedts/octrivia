# Octrivia

A one-file pixel-art arcade trivia game. Play it free in your browser:
**https://workexperiencedts.github.io/octrivia/**

Guess which **country** the octopus is hiding in: 51 countries in Chad Mode (landmarks, food, flags, sport) and 28 in Demon Time (volcanoes, deserts and heat records), each with an easy, medium and hard clue, dealt in a random order. Works on computers, phones and iPads. Single-player only in this copy.

## Two ways to play

- **PLAY:** the full game. Rescue the hostages in Chad Mode, open the gate to Demon Time on Octo Island, then beat the giant demon octopus. Miss three times and the octopus eats you.
- **DAILY OCTO:** the same 10 clues for everyone each day (5 in Chad Mode, 4 in Demon Time, then the boss). There are no lives to lose, so every clue counts. At the end you get a row of coloured squares (green for a quick right answer, yellow for a slow one, red for a miss) and a **SHARE** button. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a computer it copies your result, ready to paste into WhatsApp or Teams. Play on days in a row to build a day streak. A new puzzle unlocks at midnight. A daily you leave half-played carries on where you stopped.

## Scoring

- A right answer is worth 100, 200 or 300 points for an easy, medium or hard clue.
- On top of that is a **speed bonus** of up to the same again: the more of the clue timer is left when you fire, the bigger it is.
- **Streaks** multiply both: x2 from 3 right in a row, x3 from 6, and x4 (**ON FIRE**) from 9. A miss ends the streak.
- Bonuses: each boss hit +500, clearing Chad Mode +1,000, beating the boss +2,000, and +1,000 for every credit you have left.
- Your **top 5 scores** are kept on the device. Pass your best mid-game and you get a **NEW HIGH SCORE!** Leave the title screen alone for a few seconds and it shows the table, like an arcade machine.
- After a game over, press SHOOT (or tap) to go straight into another go, with the same hero already picked.

## Controls

- **Phones and iPads:** there is no SHOOT button.
  - Tap the screen to start and carry on, and tap an answer (or a barrel) to shoot it.
  - Held upright, the clue and a big button per barrel sit under the screen; held sideways, the screen fills the whole height.
- **Computer, or a phone held sideways:** tap or click a barrel (or its name sign) to shoot it. On a keyboard, keys 1-8 pick a barrel.
- **PAUSE** opens a menu with Resume, Retry (restart this stage), Sound and Quit. There's no Retry in the Daily Octo.
- **SOUND** opens the sound settings: on/off, volume, music, effects and a test sound.
- **iPhone tip:** in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen plays it full screen like an app.
